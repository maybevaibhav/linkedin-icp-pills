ICP

LinkedIn ICP Pills

Shows who someone is, right under their name on LinkedIn.

1. HubSpot connection

Anyone who is a contact in your HubSpot CRM gets the ICP pill. Contacts are matched by their LinkedIn URL (added by Apollo), with a name-based fallback.

How to get a HubSpot token (2 minutes, one time)
  1. In HubSpot click the gear icon (Settings) at the top right.
  2. Left menu: Integrations → Private Apps, then Create a private app.
  3. Name it LinkedIn ICP Pills.
  4. Open the Scopes tab, search contacts and tick crm.objects.contacts.read and crm.schemas.contacts.read. Read only, nothing else.
  5. Click Create app, then Show token and Copy.
  6. Paste it below and click Test & save.

2. Your own labels

Tag anyone with your own label, e.g. Top authority influencer. A person can have several labels. Tip: on LinkedIn, hover a name and click the small + to add a label without leaving the page.
